# Experiment 1: Cross-Family AI Judge Calibration & Causal Ablation in Production Builds

**Raw Anonymized Data:**
- `gates_anonymized.json`: `310` non-self real production verification gates across `107` unique named sessions + `20` null-session gates (`443` valid blind votes, `170` paired `GPT-6.2 Sol` vs. `Claude Opus 5.5` audits across `48` named paired sessions + `12` null-session paired gates, `70` contested `1–1` `Gemini 4 Neon` tie-breaks, `5` primary-error fallbacks; `12` meta-gates from the author's own residency-prep session excluded prior to export).
- `agents_md_ablation.json`: `16`-session paired causal ablation (`32` fresh Codex runs) testing whether `57 KB` of host `~/.codex/AGENTS.md` rule leakage drove `GPT-6.2 Sol`'s strictness.
- `ties_ground_truth_audit.json`: `24`-session deterministic seeded (`seed = 20261121`) **blind multi-rater AI adjudication** (`3` independent zero-context `gemini-4-neon-xhigh` subagent passes: Raters `A`, `B`, and tie-breaker `C`; zero model names; human verification pending) of `GPT-6.2 Sol BLOCK` / `Claude Opus 5.5 PASS` splits drawn from an expanded `82`-session live split pool (`11` inside the frozen `310`-gate snapshot + `13` subsequent live splits).

## Protocol, Partition & Anonymization
- Records were sorted strictly by full ISO-8601 `checked_at` timestamp **before** assigning chronological `gate_id` (`g001..g310`), `session_anon` (`s001..s107`), and per-session integer `seq_in_session` (all build titles, file paths, prompts, and raw logs omitted).
- **Complete `310`-Gate Partition:** `35` Stage 1 mechanical `BLOCK`s + `74` Stage 1-only `PASS`es + `198` completed Stage 2 panels (`110` `PASS`, `88` `BLOCK`) + `1` Stage 2 `ERROR` + `2` incomplete records = `310`.
- **Builder Family Shares (`N = 310`):** `gemini = 195` (`62.9%`), `claude = 70` (`22.6%`), `other = 25` (`8.1%`), `unknown = 20` (`6.5%`) — `14.5%` combined `other + unknown`.
- **Stage 2 (Blind Multi-Family Panel):** `GPT-6.2 Sol` (`medium`, inline `TASK.md` + `-i` images, `1.5%` call error rate) and `Claude Opus 5.5` (`medium`, clean `--setting-sources project`, tool-based `Read`/`Glob`, `13.6%` error rate) judged in parallel; on `1–1` splits or primary errors, `Gemini 4 Neon` (`high` effort, `25.7%` error rate) ran blind as tie-breaker.

## Key Findings (Recomputed via `python3 analyze_jury.py` or `python3 ../reproduce.py`)
1. **Session-Clustered Cross-Family Calibration Chasm (Zero Pseudoreplication):** Across `48` named paired sessions, `GPT-6.2 Sol` had a higher block rate than `Claude Opus 5.5` in **`30` sessions vs. `0` sessions** (`18` tied, exact sign test **`p = 1.86 × 10^-9`**; singleton-cluster sensitivity adding the `12` excluded null-session paired gates — including `g038`, the sole `Codex-PASS / Claude-BLOCK` gate — gives **`36` vs. `1`**, exact sign **`p = 5.50 × 10^-10`**). On chronological first attempts (`n = 48`), *both* judges blocked `60.4%` (`29/48`) and split **`14` vs. `0`** (exact McNemar **`p = 0.000122`**, `κ = 0.3015`). Across all `170` paired audits (`42.4%` disagreement, `κ = 0.2501`), the split was **`71` vs. `1`** (**`p = 3.09 × 10^-20`**; `158` software builds only: **`65` vs. `0`**, **`p = 5.42 × 10^-20`**).
2. **Mechanism — Proof-Completeness vs. Visible-Defect Standard:** Categorizing all `71` Codex-only blocks shows **`52/71` (`73.2%`)** were **proof-completeness objections** (*"tests/renders pass, but sub-clause X remains unproven"*) versus **`6/71` (`8.5%`)** concrete visual/data defects (`13/71` other).
3. **Paired Causal Ablation Rules Out `AGENTS.md` Leakage (`agents_md_ablation.json`):** Re-judging `16` distinct sessions (`12` Codex-only BLOCK + `4` mutual BLOCK) under isolated `CODEX_HOME` (0 bytes user rules) vs. default `~/.codex` (`57 KB` `AGENTS.md`) yielded **`11/12` vs. `11/12` BLOCK** (`1` vs. `1` flip, exact McNemar `p = 1.0`) on contested builds and **`4/4` vs. `4/4`** on mutual blocks — showing no net shift from `AGENTS.md` leakage (ruling it out as the net driver while noting single-case flips).
4. **Blind Multi-Rater AI Adjudication of 24 Unique Split Sessions (`ties_ground_truth_audit.json`, `seed = 20261121`):** Three independent zero-context `gemini-4-neon-xhigh` passes (`A`, `B`, tie-breaker `C`; zero model names; inspecting actual desktop/phone PNGs and Stage-1 logs directly; model-graded, human verification pending) adjudicated `24` unique `Codex-BLOCK / Claude-PASS` sessions (`11` inside the frozen `310`-gate snapshot + `13` subsequent live splits from the expanded `82`-session split pool):
   - **Real defects missed by `Claude Opus 5.5`:** **`3/24` (`12.5%`, 95% CI `2.7–32.4%`)** — **`1/11` (`9.1%`)** in-corpus (`T09`) and **`2/13` (`15.4%`)** post-snapshot (`T10`, `T15`) — with **`24/24` (`100.0%`, binary Cohen's `κ = 1.000`)** unanimous agreement between Raters A and B. Crucially, all `3` instances were recurring automated-loop iterations hitting **`1` unique underlying UI defect** (severely blurred, upscaled preview panels with overlapping overlay badges).
   - **Non-defect splits (`21/24 = 87.5%`):** **`15/24` (`62.5%`, CI `40.6–81.2%`)** `UNPROVEN_CLAIM_GAP` vs. **`6/24` (`25.0%`, CI `9.8–46.7%`)** `FALSE_ALARM_OR_NITPICK` by 2-of-3 consensus. All `14/24` A-vs.-B disagreements occurred strictly on this non-defect boundary (3-class `κ = 0.1962`), with `11/14` driven by opaque non-verbose dot-only `unittest` logs (`Ran N tests OK`) and discarded HTTP status-probe bodies.
5. **Tie-Breaker Alignment & Regime-Matched Self-Preference (Null):** On `70` contested `1–1` splits, `Gemini 4 Neon` voted **`PASS` on `67/70` (`95.7%`, 95% CI `88.0–99.1%`)**. Conditioning strictly on comparable decision regimes shows **no statistically significant self-preference** for either `Claude Opus 5.5` (`21/48 = 43.8%` on Claude vs. `41/107 = 38.3%` on Gemini, Fisher `p = 0.596`) or `Gemini 4 Neon` on `1–1` ties (`0/46` on Gemini vs. `2/17` on Claude, Fisher `p = 0.070`).
6. **Closed-Loop Session Recovery:** Of `41` named sessions whose first full gate was `BLOCK`, **`25/41` (`61.0%`)** later passed a gate.
