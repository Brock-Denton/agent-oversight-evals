# Experiment 1: Cross-Family AI Judge Calibration & Causal Ablation in Production Builds

**Raw Anonymized Data:**
- [`gates_anonymized.json`](gates_anonymized.json): 310 non-self real production verification gates across 107 unique named sessions + 20 null-session gates (443 valid blind votes, 170 paired GPT-6.2 Sol vs. Claude Opus 5.5 audits across 48 named paired sessions + 12 null-session paired gates, 70 contested 1–1 Gemini 4 Neon tie-breaks, 5 primary-error fallbacks; 12 meta-gates from the author's own residency-prep session excluded prior to export).
- [`agents_md_ablation.json`](agents_md_ablation.json): 16-session paired causal ablation (32 fresh Codex runs) testing whether 57 KB of host `~/.codex/AGENTS.md` rule leakage drove GPT-6.2 Sol's strictness.
- [`ties_ground_truth_audit.json`](ties_ground_truth_audit.json): 24-session deterministic seeded (`seed = 20261121`) **blind multi-rater AI adjudication** (3 independent zero-context `gemini-4-neon-xhigh` subagent passes: Raters A, B, and tie-breaker C; zero model names; human verification pending) of GPT-6.2 Sol BLOCK / Claude Opus 5.5 PASS splits drawn from an expanded 82-session live split pool (11 inside the frozen 310-gate snapshot + 13 subsequent live splits).

## Protocol, Partition & Anonymization
- Records were sorted strictly by full ISO-8601 `checked_at` timestamp **before** assigning chronological `gate_id` (`g001..g310`), `session_anon` (`s001..s107`), and per-session integer `seq_in_session` (all build titles, file paths, prompts, and raw logs omitted).
- **Complete 310-Gate Partition:** 35 Stage 1 mechanical BLOCKs + 74 Stage 1-only PASSes + **198 completed Stage 2 verdicts** (110 PASS, 88 BLOCK — comprising **173 fully resolved cross-family panels** [70 three-family resolved ties + 98 two-family unanimous Codex+Claude + 5 two-family primary-error + Gemini fallbacks], **2 fail-closed two-family 1–1 split BLOCKs** [`g121`, `g188` where Gemini errored; 175 total 2+ family completions], plus **23 single-family completions** after primary errors) + 1 Stage 2 ERROR + 2 incomplete records = 310.
- **Builder Family Shares (*N* = 310):** `gemini` = 195 (62.9%), `claude` = 70 (22.6%), `other` = 25 (8.1%), `unknown` = 20 (6.5%) — 14.5% combined `other + unknown`.
- **Stage 2 (Blind Multi-Family Panel — Full Production Judge Stacks):** GPT-6.2 Sol (`medium`, inline `TASK.md` + `-i` images, 1.5% call error rate) and Claude Opus 5.5 (`medium`, clean `--setting-sources project`, tool-based `Read`/`Glob`, 13.6% error rate) judged in parallel; on 1–1 splits or primary errors, Gemini 4 Neon (`high` effort, 25.7% error rate) ran blind as tie-breaker (failing closed to `BLOCK` on `g121` and `g188` when Gemini errored). The production comparison compares these full deployed stacks (model + CLI + tool/prompt setup), not model weights alone.

## Key Findings (Recomputed via `python3 analyze_jury.py` or `python3 ../reproduce.py`)
1. **Session-Level Stack Divergence *(descriptive directional count; does not establish task-level significance because repeated automated loops span sessions without task-level deduplication)*:** Across 48 named paired sessions, the GPT-6.2 Sol stack had a higher block rate than the Claude Opus 5.5 stack in **30 sessions vs. 0 sessions** (18 tied; conditional exact sign *p* = 1.86 × 10⁻⁹ strictly under unverified session independence; singleton-cluster sensitivity adding the 12 excluded null-session paired gates — including `g038`, the sole Codex-PASS / Claude-BLOCK gate — gives **36 vs. 1**, conditional *p* = 5.53 × 10⁻¹⁰). On the **first paired gate per named session** (*n* = 48; cross-session loop dependence still present), both judges blocked 60.4% (29/48) and split **14 vs. 0** (conditional exact McNemar *p* = 0.000122, κ = 0.3015). Descriptive gate-level totals (binomial CIs and McNemar *p*-values omitted due to repeated gates within sessions): across all 170 paired audits (42.4% disagreement, κ = 0.2501), the split was **71 vs. 1**; across the 158 `kind == "build"` paired gates (excluding the 12 `kind == "unknown"` null-session gates), **65 vs. 0**.
2. **Mechanism — Proof-Completeness vs. Visible-Defect Standard:** Author-categorized summary counts stored in `gates_anonymized.json` (`codex_only_blocker_themes`, raw gate logs withheld for anonymization) attribute **52/71 (73.2%)** Codex-only blocks to **proof-completeness objections** (*"tests/renders pass, but sub-clause X remains unproven"*) versus **6/71 (8.5%)** concrete visual/data defects (13/71 other).
3. **Paired Isolated `CODEX_HOME` Swap ([`agents_md_ablation.json`](agents_md_ablation.json)):** Recomputed directly from the 16 raw paired session rows (12 Codex-only BLOCK + 4 mutual BLOCK) comparing default `~/.codex` (incl. 57 KB `AGENTS.md` and default Codex home state) against an isolated auth-only `CODEX_HOME`: **11/12 vs. 11/12** BLOCK (2 opposing single-case flips, exact McNemar *p* = 1.0) on contested builds and **4/4 vs. 4/4** on mutual blocks — supporting only the observed net equivalence under that swap *(swapping the full `CODEX_HOME` directory on a selected sample does not isolate `AGENTS.md` alone)*.
4. **Blind Multi-Rater AI Adjudication of 24 Unique Split Sessions ([`ties_ground_truth_audit.json`](ties_ground_truth_audit.json), `seed = 20261121`):** Three zero-context `gemini-4-neon-xhigh` passes (Raters A, B, tie-breaker C; zero model names; inspecting actual desktop/phone PNGs and Stage-1 logs directly; model-graded, human verification pending; binomial CIs omitted across all categories due to repeated-loop dependence across splits) adjudicated 24 unique Codex-BLOCK / Claude-PASS sessions (11 inside the frozen 310-gate snapshot + 13 subsequent live splits from the expanded 82-session split pool):
   - **Real defects missed by Claude Opus 5.5:** **3/24 (12.5%)** — **1/11 (9.1%)** in-corpus (`T09`) and **2/13 (15.4%)** post-snapshot (`T10`, `T15`) — with **24/24 (100.0%, binary Cohen's κ = 1.000)** unanimous agreement between Raters A and B. All 3 instances were recurring automated-loop iterations hitting **1 unique underlying UI defect** (severely blurred upscaled preview panels with overlapping overlay badges).
   - **Non-defect splits (21/24 = 87.5%):** **15/24 (62.5%)** `UNPROVEN_CLAIM_GAP` vs. **6/24 (25.0%)** accurate-but-non-blocking nitpicks (stored under legacy JSON label `FALSE_ALARM_OR_NITPICK`: scoped claim satisfied, extra capture or style demand) by 2-of-3 consensus. All 14/24 A-vs-B disagreements fell on this non-defect boundary (3-class κ = 0.1962), with a descriptive **13:1 directional imbalance** between identical-model passes (Rater A 16:5 vs. Rater B 4:17; case-level McNemar *p* omitted due to cross-session loop dependence, and the counts alone do not identify a causal mechanism). Archived case records show **8/14** discordant cases involved non-verbose dot-only test logs or uncaptured probe bodies (**9/14** non-visual total, **5/14** with visual evidence).
5. **Tie-Breaker Alignment & Descriptive Builder-vs-Judge Block Rates:** Out of **72** paired Codex/Claude splits (`71 + 1`), **70** were resolved by Gemini 4 Neon and **2** (`g121`, `g188`, both Codex BLOCK / Claude PASS) defaulted fail-closed to **`BLOCK`** when Gemini errored. On the 70 resolved 1–1 splits (`46` Gemini-built, `17` Claude-built, `7` `unknown`-builder, `0` `other`-builder), Gemini 4 Neon voted **PASS on 67/70 (95.7%)**. Descriptive regime-matched block rates (gate-level Fisher *p*-values omitted because production gates repeat within sessions and compare unmatched builder workloads): Claude blocked `21/48 (43.8%)` on Claude builds vs. `41/107 (38.3%)` on Gemini builds; Gemini on 1–1 ties blocked `0/46 (0.0%)` on Gemini builds vs. `2/17 (11.8%)` on Claude builds (`1/7` on `unknown`).
6. **Closed-Loop Session Recovery:** Of 41 named sessions whose first non-dry-run gate was BLOCK (11 Stage 1 mechanical + 30 Stage 2), **25/41 (61.0%)** later passed a full Stage 2 gate.
